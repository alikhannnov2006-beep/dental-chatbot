Smile Care Dental Clinic - About
Smile Care is a family dental clinic in Karachi. We have 3 dentists and treat adults and children.

Timings
Monday to Saturday: 10am to 8pm. Sunday: closed. Emergency cases are seen on Sunday by appointment only.

Consultation and checkup
Consultation fee is Rs 1,000. If you continue treatment with us, the consultation fee is adjusted in the treatment cost.

Teeth cleaning and polishing
Scaling and polishing costs Rs 4,000. It takes about 45 minutes. Recommended every 6 months.

Fillings
Tooth-colored filling costs Rs 5,000 per tooth. Silver filling costs Rs 3,000 per tooth.

Root canal
Root canal treatment costs Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 depending on the tooth. Usually needs 2 visits.

Teeth whitening
Professional whitening costs Rs 20,000 for one session of 60 minutes.

Appointments
Book by calling 0300-0000000 or via WhatsApp on the same number. Walk-ins are accepted but appointments get priority.

Payment
We accept cash, bank transfer, and all major cards. Installments are available for treatments above Rs 30,000.
